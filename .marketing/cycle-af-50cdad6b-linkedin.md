The moment an autonomous agent actually works, you lose sight of it. You dispatch three, close the laptop to make coffee, and now three Claude Code sessions are off doing multi-hour work you are not watching. Moving? Stuck at a gate? Burning tokens on a loop? For a long time my honest answer was "I'll find out when it's done." That is a bad answer.

So I want to show off the part of agentfactory I have barely talked about = the web console. A loopback-only control room for your factory. Every running agent is a lit sign on the Floor, and the glow IS its honest status - working, gated, waiting, needs attention. You click in, read what it is doing, send it mail, or sling it a task, all from the browser.

And it just got the tab I most wanted: Telemetry. Per-step timing plus token and dollar cost, per agent. The surprise when I turned it on? The fidelity gate everyone worries about closed in 12 milliseconds. The real cost was the reading and writing. I would have guessed wrong.

You can't improve what you can't see.

github.com/stempeck/agentfactory

If you run agents on long workflows - do you have a window into them, or are you closing the laptop and hoping? Happy to help.
